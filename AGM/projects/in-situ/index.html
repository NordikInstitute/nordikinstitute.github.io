IN SITU — Place-based Innovation of Cultural and Creative Industries in Non-Urban Areas

Rethinking how cultural and creative industries strengthen non-urban places.

01 / THE BIG QUESTION

What is this research about?

The four-year IN SITU project investigated innovation in cultural and creative industries in non-urban European regions. Its themes resonate with opportunities and barriers facing Northern Ontario's creative sector.

“How can creative industries contribute to resilience and economic renewal outside major cities?”
02 / THE APPROACH

How the work comes together.

  • Combine research with experimental actions in non-urban European areas.
  • Examine barriers including access to support, training, networking and data.
  • Contribute international advice, roundtables, report reviews and conference presentations.
03 / WHAT WE'RE LEARNING

Findings & progress.

  • The project connects cultural and creative activity with identity, belonging, learning, enjoyment, tourism, economic diversification and regeneration.
  • The poster identifies obstacles that also face Northern Ontario's cultural and creative industries: limited recognition, support, access to training, networking opportunities, gender disparities and data constraints.
  • Dr. Jude Ortiz of NORDIK served as an international adviser, joining roundtables, reviewing reports and presenting at conferences. Dr. Nancy Duxbury is identified with the University of Coimbra.
✳
WHAT THIS COULD MEAN

The work offers ideas for supporting non-urban cultural communities. The poster does not quantify final economic impact.
