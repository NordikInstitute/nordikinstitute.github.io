Research with care

This project concerns Residential School Survivors, families and Indigenous community leadership. The poster contains sensitive history; content is shared here for exhibition review. Public use, imagery and descriptions require approval from the appropriate community partners. Protected database records are not made available here.

01 / THE BIG QUESTION

What is this research about?

The Children of Shingwauk Alumni Association and NORDIK's Community Support Team work to centre Survivors' voices and support investigations related to children who did not return home from Residential Schools.

“How can Survivors' truths, investigative records and community priorities be supported through respectful research and stewardship?”
02 / THE APPROACH

How the work comes together.

  • Support Survivor-led truth-telling and grounds-search work at the former Shingwauk Indian Residential School site.
  • Collaborate with the Office of the Chief Coroner of Ontario's Residential School Death Investigation Team.
  • Develop a cloud-based Shingwauk Database with specialist programming and data-stewardship practices.
03 / WHAT WE'RE LEARNING

Findings & progress.

  • The poster describes the co-development of the Shingwauk Database and historical records research conducted since 2021.
  • The poster shows a secure sign-in to the database; access to records is not represented as public or unrestricted.
✳
WHAT THIS COULD MEAN

The project aims to improve appropriately governed access to records and support community-led truth-telling. Sensitive data remain protected.
