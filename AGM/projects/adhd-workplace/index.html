From Evidence to Everyday Support: Rethinking ADHD in the Workplace

Combining research on ADHD with an in-development support app.

RESEARCH STATUSPublication in 2026; app prototype under reviewFOCUSADHD · Workplace inclusion · Public health

01 / THE BIG QUESTION

What is this research about?

Research on the economic burden of ADHD has grown, but practical workplace support for adults remains an important gap. This project combines a review of the scientific literature with the development of a workplace-oriented support tool.

“What does research on ADHD's economic burden overlook, and how might workplace support address practical needs?”
02 / THE APPROACH

How the work comes together.

  • Conduct bibliometric and thematic analysis of literature on the economic burden of ADHD, published as an article in the Journal of Public Health in 2026.
  • The poster's production chart covers 2001 to 2025 and maps themes in the literature.
  • Develop a workplace-support prototype and review it with employers who employ people with ADHD.
03 / WHAT WE'RE LEARNING

Findings & progress.

  • A related bibliometric analysis was published in the Journal of Public Health in 2026. The poster titles it Economic burden of attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder: a bibliometric and thematic analysis.
  • The poster reports that the literature remains geographically concentrated and more focused on childhood than adult ADHD.
  • Its country chart shows affiliation shares of 37.9% for the USA, 9.4% for Spain, 7.6% for England, 5.5% for Canada and 5.1% for the Netherlands.
  • A workplace app prototype is under employer review. Proposed tools include task breakdowns, priority or recency planning, guidance after mistakes, and structured coping prompts.
✳
WHAT THIS COULD MEAN

The intended benefit is more practical support for adults with ADHD at work. The poster does not establish clinical efficacy or effectiveness of the prototype.
