DATA NORTH

An accessible platform for exploring the data behind Northern communities.

01 / THE BIG QUESTION

What is this research about?

Good local decisions need trustworthy local evidence. DATA NORTH brings community indicators and historical information together in a public-facing platform, with a conversational AI assistant designed to help people interpret what the data shows.

“How can Northern Ontario communities better discover and understand local data?”
02 / THE APPROACH

How the work comes together.

  • Organize indicators across multiple categories and Northern Ontario communities.
  • Make historical information available for exploring trends.
  • Provide data exploration with conversational, source-aware assistance.
03 / WHAT WE'RE LEARNING

Findings & progress.

  • The poster presents a live NIA AI assistant and a data-exploration platform.
  • The project identifies expanding community coverage as its next step.

WHAT THIS COULD MEAN

Designed to support community development, research and evidence-based planning by making local information easier to discover.
