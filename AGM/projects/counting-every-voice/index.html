Workforce & Immigration

Counting Every Voice: Building Better Evidence for Northern Communities

Examining how immigrant surveys represent the communities they describe.

01 / THE BIG QUESTION

What is this research about?

Local immigrant surveys guide settlement planning, but they can differ substantially from population benchmarks. This study reviews Canadian municipal survey practices and explores where and why results diverge from census data.

“How accurately do local immigrant surveys reflect the people municipalities aim to serve?”
02 / THE APPROACH

How the work comes together.

  • Review 33 publicly identified local immigrant survey reports.
  • Compare sample characteristics and sizes with census benchmarks.
  • Study relationships between survey deviations and local or implementation characteristics.
03 / WHAT WE'RE LEARNING

Findings & progress.

  • Eighteen of 23 compared surveys (78%) showed major divergence from census benchmarks in at least one category.
  • The poster reports that area-level characteristics were collectively associated with survey deviations, but measured implementation characteristics were not.
  • One comparison-point statistic on the original poster contains an internal discrepancy and is withheld from the summary pending verification.

WHAT THIS COULD MEAN

The analysis contributes to better survey design and interpretation for community planning; recommendations should be read in light of the study's review status.
