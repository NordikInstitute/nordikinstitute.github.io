STOP 01
Workforce & Immigration

Connecting newcomer skills and opportunity

Northern Ontario faces workforce shortages even as newcomers bring education, qualifications and experience. This project aims to understand the gap between newcomer skills and employer needs, and to support more effective workforce strategies.

STOP 02
Workforce & Immigration

When local surveys miss part of the story

Local immigrant surveys guide settlement planning, but they can differ substantially from population benchmarks. This study reviews Canadian municipal survey practices and explores where and why results diverge from census data.

Explore this project ↗
STOP 03
Health & Wellbeing

From evidence to everyday workplace support

Research on the economic burden of ADHD has grown, but practical workplace support for adults remains an important gap. This project combines a review of the scientific literature with the development of a workplace-oriented support tool.

Explore this project ↗
STOP 04
Health & Wellbeing

What brings doctors to rural communities?

In Canada, rural and remote communities account for about 18% of the population and are served by about 8% of physicians. This research asks whether Saskatchewan's Rural Physician Incentive Program changed the rural-urban distribution of family physicians.
