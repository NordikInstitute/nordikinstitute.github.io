## Audit notes

- Source image-based spruce budworm poster was visually reviewed; its quantitative claims and team were transcribed from page 1.
- The two CST posters concern Indigenous-led community work and Residential School Survivors. Public distribution requires community permission.
- The survey methodology poster contains the conflicting 39/64 and 58% values; the online editorial summary does not propagate the conflict.
- Researcher labels preserve source variants, rather than guessing duplicate identities.
- Planned outcomes and prototype status are kept distinct from completed activities and experimentally reported findings.
- Methods, findings, metrics, partner attribution and exact original PDF mapping are editable under `content/projects.json` with a sourceNote per record.
