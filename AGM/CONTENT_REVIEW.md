## Known source discrepancy

**Counting Every Voice (Poster 2 — Online Survey Manuscript)** reports **“39 of 64 comparison points (58%)”**. The fraction `39/64 ≈ 60.94%`, not 58%. The **original PDF is untouched**. The site's human-readable project summary does **not** republish this internally inconsistent percentage. Obtain author clarification before editing source materials or adding a corrected statistic.

The same poster mentions **33** local immigrant survey reports, but describes **18 of 23** surveyed comparisons. These describe different stages/subsets in the poster; do not equate the two denominators. The PRISMA figure on the same sheet labels the final synthesis list as **n=32**, and also shows a targeted search of **n=1** feeding that list. Do not collapse 33, 32 and 18 of 23 into one count.
