01Welcome

Research that begins with community.

NORDIK Institute connects students, researchers and community in projects that contribute to social, economic and environmental sustainability and social justice for Northern and Indigenous communities. This year's Annual General Meeting gathers twelve research stories, from weather radar and water monitoring to library value, rural health care and Survivor-led truth-telling.
