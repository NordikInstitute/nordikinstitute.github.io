Water & Environment

Communities protecting their waters

Building local knowledge and capacity from Lake Superior to Lake Huron.

How can communities build the skills, evidence and partnerships to protect their own waters?
  • From 2021 to 2026, more than 200 volunteers monitored 46 sites and collected more than 45,000 data points over 465 visits.
  • More than 10,000 people were reached through more than 40 public events, and the poster reports more than 75,000 social media impressions.
  • Sixty-seven youth were mentored, trained or hired: 52 Indigenous youth interns and hires from 15 First Nations across Canada, and 15 post-secondary placements.

Five-year achievements, 2021–2026

This project concerns Residential School Survivors, families and community leadership. The summary is for the exhibition. It does not open protected records.
